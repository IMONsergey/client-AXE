# 01 — Project brief

## Client / brand

**International Association of Commodity & Futures Exchanges (ACE)** — Международная ассоциация товарных и фьючерсных бирж.

Repository: `IMONsergey/client-AXE`.

> Naming discrepancy: the repository uses `AXE`, while every supplied public-facing asset uses **ACE**. Until the client states otherwise, `AXE` is treated only as an internal repository codename. Public copy and logo must use ACE.

## Current stage

**Design concept**: define the visual language on the first blocks of the desktop homepage. This is an approval stage, not final full-page production.

## Business / perception goal

The website must visually support the Association's ambition to become a **global information infrastructure for international inter-exchange trade**.

The required perception is not “a representative NGO website”. It should feel like:

- an international market-infrastructure institution;
- a credible platform connecting exchanges and markets;
- a data-led digital product with a global operating scope;
- a premium, controlled, technically mature financial brand.

## Design brief supplied by client

The visual language should combine:

1. institutional strictness of major international associations;
2. the confidence and product language of modern financial infrastructure companies;
3. large, assured typography;
4. visible proof: numbers, scale, documents, participants and market facts;
5. a clean light base;
6. restrained, point use of ACE corporate colors;
7. calm premium presentation rather than decorative “fintech effects”.

## Concept acceptance criteria

The first-screen concept is successful only if it immediately communicates all four:

- **international scale**;
- **exchange / market infrastructure**;
- **institutional credibility**;
- **digital product maturity**.

If it can be mistaken for a generic association, consulting, bank or SaaS landing page, the concept is not strong enough.
