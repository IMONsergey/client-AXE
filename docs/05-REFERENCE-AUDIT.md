# 05 — Reference audit

The references should not be blended evenly. They serve different roles in the final system.

## WFE — primary institutional benchmark

Use for:

- international association authority;
- “future of markets” scale language;
- market statistics as proof;
- research / news / regulatory information architecture;
- data-first rather than decorative credibility.

Do **not** inherit a conservative association look if it weakens the digital-platform ambition.

## CME Group — primary market-infrastructure benchmark

Use for:

- “global marketplace / where markets operate” framing;
- dense but disciplined market-product semantics;
- serious dark modules inside an otherwise controlled system;
- navigation that can scale to many products, markets and resources.

CME is the strongest reference for making ACE feel like an operating market environment rather than a club of members.

## GAFTA — governance / trade-association benchmark

Use for:

- membership legitimacy;
- contracts, arbitration, standards, policy and documents;
- international trade-association content taxonomy.

Do not use it as the main visual benchmark; it is more useful structurally and institutionally.

## Stripe — digital infrastructure benchmark

Use for:

- “infrastructure” product language;
- confidence of large typography;
- modular systems diagrams;
- progressive disclosure of complex capabilities;
- sophisticated balance between product UI and brand storytelling.

Do not copy Stripe gradients or visual signatures. The useful donor is its information hierarchy and systems thinking.

## Ramp — product proof / modularity benchmark

Use for:

- platform-first message architecture;
- large factual claims paired with operational product modules;
- clear, modular decomposition of a complex financial system;
- disciplined UX density.

Avoid making ACE look like a SaaS expense product.

## Paystand — network narrative benchmark

Use for:

- network / rails / connected financial infrastructure metaphors;
- presenting a platform as a system that sits between existing institutions.

Useful for ACE because inter-exchange connectivity is core to the story.

## Betterment / Acorns — secondary only

Use selectively for:

- readability;
- calm spacing;
- approachable explanatory content.

They are consumer financial brands. Their emotional / lifestyle language should **not** drive ACE's identity.

## Synthesis

The target mix for ACE concept v1:

- **35% WFE / GAFTA** — legitimacy, governance, association role;
- **30% CME** — market-scale infrastructure;
- **25% Stripe / Ramp / Paystand** — modern digital product language;
- **10% Betterment / Acorns** — clarity and breathing room only.

The concept should feel closer to **“a new international market infrastructure institution”** than to either a classic trade-association website or a startup landing page.
